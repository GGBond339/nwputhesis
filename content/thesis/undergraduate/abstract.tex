\begin{abstract}
    发射逃逸系统直接关系航天员生命安全。针对载人登月任务需求，本文以猎户座飞船为参考构型，开展发射逃逸阶段六自由度动力学建模、蒙特卡洛仿真与风险评估研究。

    建立了发射逃逸六自由度动力学仿真模型，包含坐标系变换、国际标准大气（ISA）模型、马赫数分段气动模型、逃逸主发动机（AM）/姿控发动机（ACM）/抛塔发动机（JM）多发动机推进系统和比例-微分（PD）姿控律，引入风扰、推力偏差、质量偏差和气动偏差等六类干扰因素，实现了零高度逃逸和最大动压逃逸两种工况的数值仿真。

    采用$L_{27}(3^8)$正交试验和500组拉丁超立方抽样进行打靶分析。 零高度逃逸在 $50\ \mathrm{m}$ 命中半径下命中率 96.20\%，主要受风扰影响；最大动压逃逸在 $1000\ \mathrm{m}$ 命中半径下命中率 95.20\%，对气动与大气模型偏差最敏感。

    基于打靶统计结果建立了三级风险评估体系，综合命中精度、姿态稳定性和结构安全三项维度。评估表明，两种工况的命中风险和姿态风险均为低风险，超限概率均低于5\%，控制裕度与结构安全裕度充足。最大动压工况峰值动压已接近结构承载边界，尾部风险值得关注，最极端脱靶量可达 $1414.684\ \mathrm{m}$。

    \keywordslist{载人登月；逃逸系统；六自由度动力学；蒙特卡洛仿真；风险评估}
\end{abstract}

\begin{engabstract}
    The launch escape system is directly related to astronaut safety. Aiming at manned lunar mission requirements, this paper takes the Orion spacecraft as a reference and conducts six-degree-of-freedom dynamic modeling, Monte Carlo simulation and risk assessment for the launch escape phase.

    A six-degree-of-freedom simulation model was established, including coordinate transformation, International Standard Atmosphere (ISA) model, Mach-number-segmented aerodynamic model, Abort Motor (AM) / Attitude Control Motor (ACM) / Jettison Motor (JM) multi-engine propulsion system and Proportional-Differential (PD) attitude control law. Six categories of disturbance factors including wind, thrust deviation, mass deviation and aerodynamic deviation were incorporated, enabling numerical simulation of both zero-altitude escape and maximum dynamic pressure escape conditions.

    $L_{27}(3^8)$ orthogonal tests and 500 Latin hypercube sampling runs were conducted for shooting analysis. The zero-altitude escape achieved a hit rate of 96.20\% within a 50 m radius, most sensitive to wind disturbance; the maximum dynamic pressure escape achieved 95.20\% within a 1000 m radius, most sensitive to aerodynamic and atmospheric model deviations.

    A three-level risk assessment system was established covering hit accuracy, attitude stability and structural safety. Both conditions show low hit risk and low attitude risk, with exceedance probabilities below 5\% and sufficient control and structural safety margins. The peak dynamic pressure in the maximum dynamic pressure condition is close to the structural load limit, and the tail risk deserves attention, with miss distance up to 1414.684 m in extreme cases.

     \engkeywordslist{crewed lunar landing; launch abort system; six-degree-of-freedom (6-DOF) dynamics; Monte Carlo simulation; risk assessment}
\end{engabstract}
